Results worth stating because they are new here:

* **Proposition 1.** E[η²] = (1 − b)²E[o²] + E[(o\* − b o)²]. The sharp bound is
  E[η²] ≥ (1 − b)²E[o²], attained exactly when o\* = b o (for b > 0, when the error is proportional
  to the efficient open). The
  manuscript's Appendix A bound is valid but strictly weaker, and its stated attainment was wrong
  (`AUDIT-REGISTER.md` M-026).
* **Proposition 2.** Under an instantly corrected Gaussian opening error, the exact biases are:

  | Estimator | Bias |
  |---|---|
  | Rogers–Satchell | σ_c²{ρ²/2 − [(1 + ρ²)arctan ρ − ρ]/π}, in closed form |
  | Parkinson | Given by a one-dimensional integral of the Brownian extremes' joint law. It equals σ_η²/2 to within 1% for σ_η ≤ σ_c |

  The open is the session's high or low with probability (2/π)arctan(σ_η/σ_c). The loadings of
  the error's variance are:

  | Estimator | Loading |
  |---|---|
  | close-to-close | 0 |
  | open-to-close square | 1 |
  | Parkinson | ½ |
  | Rogers–Satchell | ½ |
  | Garman–Klass | 1 − ln 2 |
  | overnight² + Parkinson | 3/2 |
  | overnight² + Garman–Klass | 2 − ln 2 |
  | daily Yang–Zhang | (3 + k)/2 |
* **Proposition 3.** The measured b under a band is the latent b times
  F = E[g(o)o]/E[g(o)²] ≥ 1. For a Gaussian open, F(k) = (2Φ(k) − 1)/E[min(z², k²)], strictly
  decreasing in the band's width.

### B2. The error's scale

The share of non-stale opens at the session high or low implies a larger σ_η/σ_c than b does under
independence, in every regime:

| Regime | From the share | From b |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 0.64 | 0.43 |
| B | 0.70 | 0.55 |
| A2 | 0.78 | 0.46 |
| C | 1.05 | 0.96 |

As the plan fixed beforehand, this reads as the open being an extreme more often than an instantly
corrected Gaussian error explains: thin books, or slow correction. Both estimates rise at the
reform.

### B3. Censoring

* **The factor.** Under the ±2% band the Gaussian censoring factor is 1.21–1.30. The observed
  b/b_interior is 1.21 in A1, 1.15 in B and 1.36 in A2. The observed values fall on both sides of
  the Gaussian ones.
* **The decomposition.** NEPSE's fall in b from 0.356 (A2) to 0.130 (C) splits exactly into three
  log terms:

  | Term | Log | Share |
  |---|---|---|
  | Censoring under the old band | 0.311 | 31% |
  | Change in the interior coefficient | 0.044 | 4% |
  | Departure from linearity in C | 0.649 | 65% |

  The last term is positive. Under a linear latent conditional mean that is impossible, so large
  opens overshoot after the widening.

### B4. The calibration after the reform

* **The ratios.** ρ₀ = 0.970 (the 60 dates before the reform) and ρ₁ = 0.670 (regime C).
* **The lag path.** It explains 99% of the squared deviation of the applied calibration from ρ₁
  over C's 90 sessions (RMSE 0.017). Linear weights explain 98%.
* **The balancing window.**
  * The noise per date is v = 0.16.
  * NEPSE's three regime boundaries changed the calibration by δ = −0.103, 0.156 and −0.299 over
    567 sessions.
  * Together these give L\* ≈ 46 dates.
* **The loss gap.** The lag alone predicts that a 60-date calibration loses 0.002 more than a
  10-date one in C. The observed QLIKE gaps are 0.047 and 0.053, so the lag accounts for about 5%
  of them. Most of the gap comes from the ratio moving within C, because the estimator's own
  pooled b was still falling, and Proposition 5 holds that ratio fixed.

the frozen tables' losses exactly, to 1e-9.

* **P4a (mechanism): not supported.**
  * The open-free form had the lower loss in all ten cases, and the lower level loss in all ten.
    The pooling channel is present everywhere.
  * The prediction also required the full form to keep the better shape. That held in only two
    cases. In eight, the full form's shape loss was higher too.
  * Reading a noisy open costs more than calibration.
* **P4b (cross-section): not supported.**
  * Confirmed in four cases: NEPSE and Dhaka 2023–2026, both horizons.
  * Not detected in five.
  * Reversed in one: Dhaka 2009–2021 at five sessions.
* **An unpredicted pattern.** The prediction held where the pooled training-span b is lowest (0.28
  and 0.37) and failed where it is 0.55–0.71. This is reported only, not tested.
